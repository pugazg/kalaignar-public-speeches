# Independence Golden Jubilee — Send-off for Tamil Nadu Freedom Fighters

Karunanidhi addresses freedom fighters being sent from Tamil Nadu to Delhi to participate in the Independence Golden Jubilee celebrations. He opens by saying his gratitude is not merely for their attendance at the function, but for their role in the struggle that won freedom and raised the flag of independence.

He describes the Tamil Nadu government as one that honours, praises and assists freedom fighters. Although his age had prevented him from taking part personally in the freedom struggle, he says he considers it a privilege to have the opportunity, while in office, to honour those who did.

Karunanidhi recalls the Silver Jubilee of independence, when he was also Chief Minister. At that time he had the opportunity to present copper-plate honours to Periyar, Rajaji, Kamaraj and other leaders and to send off freedom fighters travelling to Delhi. He stresses that the government's respect for those who sacrificed for the country and language is not confined to anniversary celebrations.

He recalls earlier commemorations, including an official birthday celebration for Gomathi Sankara Dikshitar, the platinum-jubilee function for Sengalvarayan, the recovery and installation of the oil press that V. O. Chidambaranar had been forced to pull in prison, and the creation of Valliammai Nagar at Thillaiyadi with housing areas named for Narayanasamy and Nagappan, who had struggled alongside Gandhi in South Africa.

Karunanidhi says that sending the freedom fighters to Delhi is itself an honour for the people and government who are able to salute them. The gifts arranged for the travellers are described not as repayment for sacrifice but as tokens of affection and remembrance: khadi clothes, travel arrangements, accommodation at Tamil Nadu House, medical support, second-class air-conditioned rail travel and one thousand rupees for personal expenses in Delhi.

He closes by urging everyone to preserve India's sovereignty, unity and strength, to carry in their hearts the ideal “ஒன்றே குலம் ஒருவனே தேவன்,” and to remember Bharati's vision that all are sovereign citizens of the country. The final call is for equality and social justice to flourish.
