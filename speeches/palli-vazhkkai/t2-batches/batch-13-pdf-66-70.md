Thus the old-glyph readings `மாட்டானு`, `ஒன்றுக`, and `நன்றுக` must not survive T3.

## Traditional-glyph safeguard

This batch again confirms that apparent T1 endings such as `-னு`, `-னுக`, and `-க` can be visual misreadings of traditional forms representing underlying `-னா`, `-னாக`, and `-கா` sequences. In this batch the scan establishes `மாட்டானா`, `ஒன்றாக`, `நன்றாக`, and `தோழனாகவே`.
