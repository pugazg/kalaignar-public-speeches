# பள்ளி வாழ்க்கை — traditional Tamil glyph recheck (PDF 36-40)

**Scope:** T2 Batch 7, PDF pages 36-40 / printed pages 35-39  
**Status:** corrective glyph-interpretation sweep complete  
**Source authority:** supplied 1952 scan

## Confirmed correction to the T2 interpretation

On PDF 36 / printed 35, Batch 7 had changed:

- `நானா?` → `நானு?`

That change is withdrawn. The printed form is the traditional pre-1978 glyph for `னா`, not modern `னு`.

Correct source-character reading:

- `“என்ன? நானா? உனக்கா,`

The original T1 `நானா?` was correct.

## Rule reaffirmed

Traditional glyph shape is not a separate spelling. Encode the underlying Tamil character sequence (`னா`, `றா`, `ணா`, `னை`, `லை`, etc.) rather than a visually similar modern sequence.

The remaining Batch 7 corrections (`தாங்கள் தான்`, `ஏகலவனை மறுபடி`, `பக்திக்கு`, `சீறிச் சினந்து`) are unaffected by this glyph correction.
