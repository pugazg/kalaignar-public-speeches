### Confirmed E2 corrections

1. **PDF 20 / printed p.19 — omitted departure verb restored.**  
   E1 ended the A.S.A. Sami paragraph with `received in our presence the award that had been presented to him.` The frozen Tamil ends `அந்த விருதினைப் பெற்றுச் சென்றிருக்கின்றார்கள்`, which includes his leaving after receiving it. Corrected to: `received in our presence the award that had been presented to him, and left.`

2. **PDF 21 / printed p.20 — singular source wording restored.**  
   E1 rendered `கொள்கையைப் பரப்புகின்ற` as `spread the principles`. The frozen Tamil is singular. Corrected to: `spread the principle`.

3. **PDF 25 / printed p.24 — `அழைத்தார்` relation corrected.**  
   E1 said Tabla Muthukrishnan `came to Erode saying that A.S.A. Sami had invited me and took me with him.` The frozen Tamil says he came to Erode saying A.S.A. Sami was calling for the speaker and `என்னை அழைத்தார்`; it does not state that Muthukrishnan physically took him away. Corrected to: `He came to Erode saying that A.S.A. Sami had asked for me and invited me.`

4. **PDF 28 / printed p.27 — passive source relationship restored.**  
   E1 said `The testimonial from Thuglak has mentioned the Washington Thirumanam that he wrote.` The frozen Tamil has the work as the thing `குறிப்பிடப்பட்டிருக்கின்றது`. Corrected to: `The Washington Thirumanam that he wrote is mentioned in the Thuglak testimonial.`

5. **PDF 31 / printed p.30 — added agency removed.**  
   E1 said `the kind of talented people Tamil Nadu has been given...`, which introduced an unstated giver. The frozen Tamil `எப்படிப்பட்ட ஆற்றலாளர்கள் தமிழகத்திலே ... கிடைத்திருக்கிறார்கள்` does not identify such an agent. Corrected to: `the kind of talented people there are in Tamil Nadu...`.

6. **PDF 33 / printed p.32 — closing speech act made direct.**  
   E1 ended `Long live them! Long live them! That is my wish.` The frozen Tamil is explicitly first-person: `அவர்களை வாழ்க! வாழ்க! என்று வாழ்த்துகின்றேன்.` Corrected to: `I wish them, “Long live! Long live!”`
