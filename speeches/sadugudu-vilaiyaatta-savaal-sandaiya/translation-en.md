#### A Game of Sadugudu?
#### A Challenge Fight?

Chakravarti Rajagopalachari, fifteen years ago, sat as Premier of the ministry formed by Congress in the Madras Province.

After that, five years ago, some Congressmen and the Congress high command in Delhi attempted once again to make him Premier.

That this attempt could not succeed and collapsed because of the condition of Congress in the Madras Province at the time is an old story known to everyone.

Achariyar, who had been removed from this province and from the political life of this province, was appointed Governor of the West Bengal Province. After that he became the first Indian Governor-General of this Union.

After this, for some time, he served as a minister without portfolio in the Central Government.

### PDF page 30 — printed page 29

After Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel died, the post of Home Minister that he had held was given to Achariyar.

During the past five years, Achariyar obtained all the posts mentioned above.

Finally, while he was Home Minister in the Central Government, a general election was once again held throughout the country.

At this juncture Achariyar himself said that he did not want his ministerial post in the Central Government, returned to Madras and went to Courtallam. He took up a cool residence there and was writing *Bhaja Govindam*.

It is a fact known throughout the country that Achariyar himself withdrew from public life, politics and ministerial office only after declaring that he had taken political renunciation.

Achariyar did not take part in the general election. Though many asked him, though many, many Congressmen asked him, he refused, saying he could not, and went away to Courtallam.

In this situation, the general election in the country came to an end.

According to the result of this election, the number of those who stood for election in the name of Congress and won was very small.

Congress no longer had sufficient strength—the majority strength—to form a ministry in Madras.

To escape this difficult situation and once again establish Congress Party rule in Madras, members of the Congress Party and its supporters grew intensely agitated.

A party without a majority cannot form a ministry, can it? What was to be done? Congressmen yearned and suffered as they looked at the number of members in their legislative party in Madras.

If the prestige of Congress was to be saved, the Congress Party had to form the ministry in Madras.

And if the Congress Party was to form the ministry, it had to seek strength—more strength, new strength, combined strength, manpower—by increasing the number of members in the party. That was the situation!

Now Achariyar's name was once again spoken of strongly, by many.

Some Congress leaders went to Achariyar and asked him to accept the leadership of the Legislative Congress Party and form a Congress ministry.

Accordingly, the Legislative Congress Party passed a resolution requesting Achariyar to accept the leadership of the Legislative Congress Party, and it was sent to him.

Even this was not enough. He replied that they should find out whether the high command agreed to it and whether they too wanted him—

On one side, Achariyar tries to make the people believe that his nomination—his appointment as a member of the Upper House—is proper by offering reasons without reason, such as his age.

It must not be forgotten that Governor Sri Prakasa appointed Achariyar as a member of the Upper House only one hour—just one hour—before he was elected leader of the Legislative Congress Party.

Recall also the newspaper report that, when Sri Prakasa left Delhi, when he left his ministerial post in the Central Government, beloved Achariyar had made a request that he come as Governor of Madras.

After this, Sri Prakasa said at a meeting that Achariyar was his teacher, his guru.
