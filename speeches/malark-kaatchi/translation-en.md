## Flower Show

Last year circumstances prevented me from attending the flower show. I trust that all the flowers that withered then are blooming now. When I say “flowers” here, I mean your hearts. This flower show has been arranged with great beauty.

Some flowers have fragrance; some do not. Some are beautiful without fragrance. Others have fragrance without beauty.

And when one stretches out a hand to pluck a flower that has both beauty and fragrance, a thorn may prick it.

The poor, humble, oppressed and backward people of this country are like flowers without fragrance. Yet they possess good hearts. Our Government has assumed the responsibility of bringing fragrance into lives that have been denied it.

These are the thoughts that arise in me as I see this flower show. Though I am in Ooty, where flowers sway in abundance, my thoughts circle the parched district of Ramanathapuram. Likewise, when I see wealthy people living in mansions and towers, I think of those who live by the roadside—

## PDF 120 / printed p.119

in mud and poverty.

As the Nilgiri hills stand clothed in a green sari, I remember Ramanathapuram. In the same way, seeing the affluent makes me think of the poor.

Our policy is not that the Nilgiris should become like Ramanathapuram. It is that dry Ramanathapuram should become as prosperous as the Nilgiris.

Nor is our policy that the prosperous rich should become poor. It is that the poor too should gain a prosperous life.

That is why, in Anna's path, our ministers, friends, comrades and I are working. We greatly need your cooperation.
