## Official catalogue identity

The Tamil Digital Library `ஒலிப்பேழை` catalogue lists two separate Kalaignar recordings:

1. `சென்னை கலைவாணர் அரங்கம் திறப்புவிழா நிகழ்ச்சி கலைஞர் உரை`
2. `கலைவாணர் என்.எஸ்.கிருஷ்ணன் நினைவு நாள் விழா உரை`

The present archive follows the second catalogue identity: a Kalaivanar N. S. Krishnan memorial-day speech, distinct from the opening-ceremony speech.

Catalogue:

- https://tamildigitallibrary.in/kalaignar/05.kalappezhai/audio.html

## Earlier event recalled inside the recording

The current T1 says that, when the venue was still called `சிறுவர் அரங்கம்` or `பாலர் அரங்கம்`, Udumalai Narayana Kavirayar was honoured there with a shawl, a gold purse, and more than approximately ₹15,000; Kalaignar then announced that the renovated venue would take the name `கலைவாணர் அரங்கம்`.

A secondary historical account independently records that:

- a Kalaivanar memorial-day function was held at Chennai Balar Arangam on **4 September 1971**;
- Udumalai Narayana Kavirayar received a **₹15,000** gold purse;
- Kalaignar announced that Balar Arangam would be named Kalaivanar Arangam.

Source:

- `தந்தை பெரியார், கருணானந்தம்/019-021`, Wikisource: https://ta.wikisource.org/s/axie

### Archival interpretation

This is strong contextual corroboration for the **earlier event described within the recording**. It is **not evidence that the present recording was made on 4 September 1971**. The speaker refers to that honour and naming announcement retrospectively.

## Renovated hall chronology

A historical account of the old auditorium states that:

- the building had earlier served as the Madras Legislative Assembly;
- it later became Balar Arangam;
- after modernisation it was inaugurated as Kalaivanar Arangam by Chief Minister M. Karunanidhi on **29 January 1974**.

Source:

- `Curtains down on landmark auditorium Kalaivanar Arangam`, *The Times of India*, 26 November 2009: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/curtains-down-on-landmark-auditorium-kalaivanar-arangam/articleshow/5269541.cms

A separate retrospective also states that the government-owned hall was enlarged, renovated, made into a major auditorium, and named Kalaivanar Arangam by Karunanidhi.

Source:

- `நகைச்சுவை ராஜா... நாகரீகக் கோமாளி...`, *Hindu Tamil Thisai*, 30 August 2020: https://www.hindutamil.in/news/cinema/tamil-cinema/572459-kalaivanar-nsk.html

## Cautious date inference

The current recording speaks of the newly created hall as already standing and functioning in Chennai, and the official catalogue distinguishes this memorial-day speech from the hall-opening speech.

Therefore the safest contextual inference is:

> **The present memorial-day recording is likely from 29 January 1974 or later.**

This is an inference from the provisional transcript plus secondary chronology. It is not a source-stated date, and `metadata.json` must continue to keep `speech.date` as `null` unless stronger event-specific evidence is found.
