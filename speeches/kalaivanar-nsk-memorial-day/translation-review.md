## Batch 1 — 00:00–01:11

**Result:** complete. No clause, name, salutation or argument movement was omitted, added or reversed.

### Salutations

The source order remains intact:

1. Chairman of the Kalaivanar Memorial Committee / A. L. Srinivasan;
2. Chinna Annamalai;
3. distinguished members of the world of arts;
4. mothers;
5. friends.

`தாய்மார்களே` remains “mothers.” Replacing it with “ladies” would erase Kalaignar's kinship-based public salutation. The repeated `அருமை நண்பர்` remains “my dear friend” for both named addressees.

### Memorial-function paragraph

Accepted corrections:

- `மெத்த உணர்ச்சிப் பெருக்கோடும்`
  - first pass: `with a profound surge of feeling`
  - consolidated: `with a great surge of emotion`
- `அவர் விட்டுச் சென்ற பணிகளைத் தொடர்ந்து செய்திட வேண்டும்`
  - first pass: `the tasks he left behind must be carried forward`
  - consolidated: `the work he left behind must continue to be carried out`

The annual repetition, remembrance of Kalaivanar and obligation to continue his work remain preserved.

### Gratitude sentence

Accepted correction:

- first pass: `discharge the gratitude we owe Kalaivanar`
- consolidated: `express the gratitude we owe to Kalaivanar`

The complete structure remains:

- `இந்த ஒரு வகையிலேதான்` → `only in this one way`;
- `நாம் செலுத்த வேண்டிய நன்றியினை` → `the gratitude we owe`;
- `சுட்டிக்காட்டாமல் இருக்க இயலாது` → `I cannot refrain from pointing out`.

## Batch 2 — 01:11–03:15

### Household family and `கலைக்குடும்பம்`

The contrast remains:

- `the few people within the house that he built and lived in`;
- `Kalaivanar's family is the family of the arts`.

“The family of the arts” is intentionally retained instead of a smoother phrase such as “artistic community,” because Kalaignar's repeated family metaphor is structurally important.

### Decline and lamp imagery

The paired source repetition `நலிந்துபோகின்ற ... நலிந்துபோகாமல்` remains visible as `falling into hardship ... from falling further`.

`வாடி வதங்காமல் வாழ்விக்க` remains `to enable those who are withering and wasting away to live`.

The two lamp images remain distinct:

- `பெரிய ஒளிவிளக்குகள்` → `great lamps of radiance`;
- `சிறிய அகல்விளக்குகள்` → `small earthen lamps`.

Accepted correction:

- `அவர்களுடைய இல்லத்திலும்`
  - first pass: `in their homes`
  - consolidated: `in their homes as well`

### Friends, comrades and honour

- worthy of his trust;

### Udumalai Narayana Kavirayar and gifts

Accepted amount correction:

This preserves the source's approximation-plus-excess meaning without inventing a precise amount.

### Hall names and announcement

## Batch 3 — 03:15–05:42

**Result:** reviewed in full. No event, name, amount, quotation or argument movement is omitted. No outside history is inserted and no meaning is reversed.

### Reconstruction and ₹25 lakh passage — 03:15

The Tamil builds one extended sentence around two parallel reasons:

1. the former hall was not sufficiently suitable or worthy;
2. it needed to be arranged for art programmes, music programmes and many other public programmes in Chennai.

The first-pass English separated the first reason into its own sentence and began the second with `And because`. This preserved the information but weakened Kalaignar's paired `காரணத்தாலும் ... காரணத்தாலும்` construction.

Accepted structural correction:

> Because the hall that had stood here before this name was bestowed was not of sufficient standard, and because we thought it should be designed in a manner suited to the art programmes, music programmes and many other public programmes held in the city of Chennai, this hall was newly constructed at a cost of approximately twenty-five lakh rupees, and Kalaivanar's name was placed upon it.

This restores:

- the two parallel reasons;
- the single-sentence accumulation;
- `சற்றொப்ப இருபத்தைந்து இலட்ச ரூபாய்` as `approximately twenty-five lakh rupees`;
- the new construction of the hall;
- `கலைவாணருடைய பெயர் ஏற்றி வைக்கப்பட்டது` as `Kalaivanar's name was placed upon it`.

“Placed upon it” is retained rather than flattened to “it was named after Kalaivanar,” because the source contains the more visual `பெயர் ஏற்றி வைக்கப்பட்டது` construction.

### Tamil society, the arts and the majestic hall

The paired subjects remain explicit:

- Tamil society;

`பொருட்படுத்தவில்லை` remains `paid no heed`, and the hall `standing here in majesty` remains the visible sign that their objection was disregarded. Kalaignar's declaration of immense pride is retained.

### Tirukutralam auditorium — 04:44

The English preserves:

- construction of an auditorium at Tirukutralam;
- officials considering its name;
- Kalaignar's visit to Tirunelveli district while he was Minister for Public Works;
- Tirukutralam as a place very dear to Kalaivanar;
- the suggestion that the auditorium also be called Kalaivanar Arangam;
- the concluding `திகழ்கிறது` image as `also shines as Kalaivanar Arangam`.

The sentence `I went to Tirunelveli district during the period when I was Minister for Public Works` correctly avoids implying that he held a district-specific ministerial office.
