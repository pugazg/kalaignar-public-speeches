## Mandatory fidelity checks

- Preserve Kalaignar's formal public-speaking voice.
- Preserve salutation order.
- Preserve rhetorical repetition rather than compressing it for style.
- Preserve the argument sequence and paragraph correspondence.

### Terms and images

- Confirm that household `குடும்பம்` and wider `கலைக்குடும்பம்` remain distinct.
- Review “the family of the arts” as the rendering of `கலைக்குடும்பம்`.
- Preserve the contrast between great lamps of radiance and small earthen lamps.

### Source-complete ending

The final approximately 25 seconds must be present in full. Review these three movements separately:

1. the audience has seen the condition of Thangappan and Bhagavathi;

There must be no em dash, truncation note or invented missing continuation.

## Known first-pass choices requiring review

These are review points, not predetermined errors:

1. `தாய்மார்களே` → “mothers” — assess whether literal retention best preserves the public salutation.
2. `நன்றியினை ... செலுத்திட` → “discharge the gratitude we owe” — check tone and naturalness without weakening the obligation.
3. The repeated `நலிந்துபோகின்ற ... நலிந்துபோகாமல்` construction — ensure the English does not erase the repetition.
4. `பொன்னாடை`, `பொற்கிழி`, and `பணமுடிப்பு` — ensure three related but distinct gift expressions remain distinguishable.
5. `பெயர் ஏற்றி வைக்கப்பட்டது` → “the name was placed upon it” — check closeness to the spoken image.
6. `நான் நெல்லை மாவட்டத்திற்கு, பொதுப்பணித்துறை அமைச்சராக இருந்த காலத்தில், சென்றேன்` — ensure the English does not imply that he was minister only for Tirunelveli district.
7. `வள்ளல் தன்மை` → “munificent spirit” — check whether the ethical and beneficent force is retained.
8. `எந்தக் கோலத்திலே` → “the condition in which...” — check that the gravity of the spoken phrase remains.
9. `நிம்மதிதான் ... காணிக்கை` — ensure the final ethical dedication remains the speech's conclusion and is not flattened into generic charity language.
