## Known first-pass choices requiring review

These are review points, not predetermined errors:

1. `தாய்மார்களே` → “mothers” — assess whether literal retention best preserves the public salutation.
2. `நன்றியினை ... செலுத்திட` → “discharge the gratitude we owe” — check tone and naturalness without weakening the obligation.
3. The repeated `நலிந்துபோகின்ற ... நலிந்துபோகாமல்` construction — ensure the English does not erase the repetition.
4. `பொன்னாடை`, `பொற்கிழி`, and `பணமுடிப்பு` — ensure three related but distinct gift expressions remain distinguishable.

7. `வள்ளல் தன்மை` → “munificent spirit” — check whether the ethical and beneficent force is retained.

## Review findings

### Batch 1 — 00:00–01:11

**Result:** reviewed in full. No clause, name, salutation or argument movement is omitted. No idea is added, reversed or historically expanded.

#### 1. Salutations — 00:00

The English preserves the exact source order:

1. Chairman of the Kalaivanar Memorial Committee / A. L. Srinivasan;
2. Chinna Annamalai;
3. distinguished members of the world of arts;
4. mothers;
5. friends.

`தாய்மார்களே` remains “mothers.” Although “ladies” would be more conventional contemporary English, it would erase Kalaignar's kinship-based public salutation. The literal rendering is therefore retained.

The repeated `அருமை நண்பர்` is preserved as “my dear friend” for both named addressees. No correction is required.

#### 2. Memorial-function paragraph — 00:18

The first pass preserved the annual repetition and the two linked sources of feeling: remembrance of Kalaivanar and eagerness to continue his work.

Two corrections were accepted and consolidated:

- `மெத்த உணர்ச்சிப் பெருக்கோடும்`:
  - first pass: `with a profound surge of feeling`
  - consolidated: `with a great surge of emotion`
  - reason: `emotion` is closer to `உணர்ச்சி`, while `great` preserves the force of `மெத்த` without adding the interpretive shade carried by `profound`.

- `அவர் விட்டுச் சென்ற பணிகளைத் தொடர்ந்து செய்திட வேண்டும்`:
  - first pass: `the tasks he left behind must be carried forward`
  - consolidated: `the work he left behind must continue to be carried out`
  - reason: the revised wording more explicitly preserves both `தொடர்ந்து` and the obligation in `வேண்டும்`.

`நான் மிகவும் மகிழ்ச்சியடைகின்றேன்` remains `I am exceedingly happy to witness it.` The degree and formal cadence are adequately preserved.

#### 3. Gratitude sentence — 00:55

The first-pass phrase `discharge the gratitude we owe` was semantically understandable but unnatural and unnecessarily financial in tone.

Accepted correction:

- first pass: `it is only in this manner that we can discharge the gratitude we owe Kalaivanar`
- consolidated: `it is only in this one way that we can express the gratitude we owe to Kalaivanar`

The revision preserves:

- `இந்த ஒரு வகையிலேதான்` → `only in this one way`;
- `நாம் செலுத்த வேண்டிய நன்றியினை` → `the gratitude we owe`;
- `சுட்டிக்காட்டாமல் இருக்க இயலாது` → `I cannot refrain from pointing out`.

No addition or weakening was introduced.

### Batch 2 — 01:11–03:15

**Result:** reviewed in full. The English preserves the complete argument: Kalaivanar's family extends beyond the household to the wider family of the arts; the memorial day must sustain struggling artists; an earlier honour to Udumalai Narayana Kavirayar is recalled; and the former Siruvar/Balar Arangam is identified before the renaming announcement.

No clause, name, gift, amount or hall name is omitted. No historical explanation is added and no meaning is reversed.

#### 1. Household family and `கலைக்குடும்பம்` — 01:11

The contrast is correctly retained:

- `அவர் கட்டி வாழ்ந்த அந்த வீட்டுக்குள்ளே இருக்கின்ற ஒரு சிலர்` → `the few people within the house that he built and lived in`;
- `கலைவாணருடைய குடும்பம் என்பது கலைக்குடும்பம்` → `Kalaivanar's family is the family of the arts`.

“The family of the arts” is intentionally less idiomatic than “artistic community.” It preserves Kalaignar's repeated family metaphor and the direct lexical relation between `குடும்பம்` and `கலைக்குடும்பம்`. No correction is required.

#### 2. Decline, withering and preservation

The paired repetition `நலிந்துபோகின்ற ... நலிந்துபோகாமல்` remains visible as:

> `protect anyone who is falling into hardship from falling further`

The wording is somewhat literal, but that literalness preserves the source's repetition. Smoothing it into a single generic phrase such as “help struggling artists” would weaken Kalaignar's rhetoric and was rejected.

`வாடி வதங்காமல் வாழ்விக்க` remains `to enable those who are withering and wasting away to live`. The two-stage image is retained rather than compressed.

#### 3. Lamp imagery

The first pass retained the contrast between:

- `பெரிய ஒளிவிளக்குகள்` → `great lamps of radiance`;
- `சிறிய அகல்விளக்குகள்` → `small earthen lamps`.

One correction was accepted:

- first pass: `in their homes`
- consolidated: `in their homes as well`

Reason: the enclitic force in `அவர்களுடைய இல்லத்திலும்` includes the sense of “in their homes too/as well.” The revised wording restores that inclusion without changing the image.

The contrast in scale remains intact: even when great lamps cannot be lit, at least small earthen lamps should be lit.

#### 4. Friends and comrades of the world of arts

The English preserves the source's parallel descriptions:

- `நெருங்கிப் பழகிய` → `who had moved closely with Kalaivanar`;
- `அன்போடு பழகிய` → `who had moved with him in affection`;
- `விசுவாசத்திற்கு ... பாத்திரமாக இருந்த` → `worthy of Kalaivanar's trust`;
- `பெருமதிப்பிற்குப் பாத்திரமாக இருந்த` → `worthy of Kalaivanar's ... high regard`;
- `மரியாதை` → `honour`.

The repeated `பழகிய` construction remains repeated rather than being reduced to one smoother clause. No correction is required.

#### 5. Udumalai Narayana Kavirayar and the three honours — 02:21

The name and role are complete. The English keeps `இந்த இடத்திலேதான்` as `in this very place` and retains the three distinct honour/gift expressions:

1. `பொன்னாடை போர்த்தி` → `draped him in a golden shawl`;
2. `பொற்கிழியும் வழங்கினோம்` → `presented him with a gold purse`;
3. `பணமுடிப்பும் ... அளிக்கப்பட்டது` → `a cash purse ... was also given to him`.

The terms remain separate; the cash purse is not merged into the gold purse, and the ceremonial shawl is not omitted.

#### 6. ₹15,000-plus amount

The first pass read:

> `A cash purse amounting to more than approximately fifteen thousand rupees was also given to him.`

This preserved every component mechanically but produced strained English. The Tamil itself combines approximation (`ஏறத்தாழ`) with an amount exceeding fifteen thousand (`ரூபாய்க்குமேல்`).

Accepted correction:

> `A cash purse of a little over fifteen thousand rupees was also given to him.`

“A little over” communicates both approximation and excess without inventing a precise figure. The separate cash purse and `also` remain preserved.

#### 7. Former hall names and renaming announcement — 02:51

The English correctly preserves:

- `சிறுவர் அரங்கம் அல்லது பாலர் அரங்கம்` → `Siruvar Arangam, or Balar Arangam`;
- `அந்த விழாவிலேதான்` → `at that very function`;
- `இந்த இடம் புதுப்பிக்கப்பெற்று` → `this place will be renovated`;
- `பெயரை விரைவிலே சூட்டிக்கொள்ளும்` → `will soon take on the name Kalaivanar Arangam`.

The announcement remains future-facing and is not rewritten as though the renaming had already occurred. No correction is required.
