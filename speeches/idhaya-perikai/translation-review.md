## E2 review rule

Each English page was checked independently against the frozen Tamil for:

- omitted clauses or sentences;
- added ideas or editorial inference presented as source text;
- reversed meaning;
- softened or strengthened rhetoric;
- lost repetition;
- incorrect subjects, pronouns, names or titles;
- silently normalized difficult Tamil;
- page-boundary loss or duplication;
- translator/source notes that overstate what the Tamil establishes.

The review does not rewrite merely for style. A page with no fidelity issue is recorded as a pass.
