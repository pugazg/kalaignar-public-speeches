## E2 review rule

Review every English body page independently against the frozen Tamil layer. Check specifically for:

- omitted clauses or sentences;
- added ideas or explanatory content presented as if it came from the source;
- reversed meaning;
- rhetoric that has been softened or strengthened;
- repetition accidentally removed;
- incorrect subjects, pronouns, names or titles;
- silently normalized difficult Tamil;
- page-boundary loss or duplicated material;
- translator inference presented as source fact;
