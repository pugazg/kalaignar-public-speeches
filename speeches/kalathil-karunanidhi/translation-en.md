#### Karunanidhi in the Field!

Beloved Chairman! Comrades! Elders! Friends of the opposition parties who are standing aside and listening to the speech through the loudspeaker! My greetings to all of you, with thanks.

This meeting, convened here today under the auspices of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, has been called in connection with the coming general election: to support Comrade **Albert Jesudhas**, who is contesting the Madras Legislative Assembly from the North Madras Washermenpet constituency after signing an agreement affirming the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam's life-blood question—the separation of Dravida Nadu, the fundamental demand that “Dravida Nadu belongs to the Dravidians”—and thereby receiving the official support and cooperation of our Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam; and, likewise, to support Comrade **James Bond**, who, after signing the same agreement and receiving our support, is contesting for Parliament, the People's House in Delhi, from the constituency covering Washermenpet, Ponneri Taluk, Sriperumbudur, Tiruvallur, Saidapet and other areas—

—and to ask every one of you to cast your vote for them in the coming election, so that through this the cry, the heart-song, “Dravida Nadu for the Dravidians,” may resound in both the Legislative Assembly and the People's House.

Many parties and independents are contesting the election. Among them, in particular, is the Congress Party, which today occupies the seat of government.

Congress has been governing the country for more than four years. It is contesting the election again in order to sit once more on the seat of power.

Many comrades who spoke before me described what the present rulers, the Congress government, have done in these four years. What have they achieved? What good have they done for the people? They set out all the cruelties, horrifying scenes, hunger, starvation and famine that have been heaped upon the people.

Anna, the General Secretary of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, at the recently held State Conference, clearly explained the Kazhagam's—

—decision, position and programme concerning the election.

With regard to this election, he explained the aim of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam: from the time the present rulers, the present Congress government, climbed onto the seat of power until today, throughout these four years, they have brought decline upon the country. How many kinds of harm have surrounded the country! He explained how many more forms of ruin and tangled problems would surround the country if the same Congress came to power again and again, and if the grip of government once more remained trapped in its hands.

Therefore, Congress must be defeated; there must not again be the miserable condition of suffering under its disorderly rule—nor is there any need for it. This must be prevented so that the country may prosper and its people may live well.

The country knows well the cruelties committed by this Congress in the past. Many have spoken about them and continue to do so. I will tell you some things that some may have forgotten to mention, that you may have missed reading in the newspapers, or that others were unable to say. Listen.

Five years ago, T. K. Pattammal sang of dancing and singing because joyous freedom had arrived, while Baby Kamala danced in expressive gesture. From the beginning of that “joyous freedom” until today, what have these rulers done? What have they actually achieved? They have flung repression upon repression. I wish to explain this particularly, with figures.

It was during these five years of rule by the apostles of nonviolence that firearms were used **1,982** times. Not only that: **3,784** people were shot dead; more than ten thousand were seriously wounded. More than about fifty thousand were imprisoned, as preventive detainees and political prisoners. And it did not stop there. Of the political prisoners confined in jail, **eighty-two** were shot dead within the prison bars themselves.

Where did all this happen? Who are these arrogant men who, within five years, used firearms one thousand nine hundred and eighty-two times? Hitler? No, no! Mussolini? Certainly not! Then who? The Tsar? No. They are all the heirs of nonviolence, Gandhi's foremost disciples, now enthroned on the seat of government—

A horrific sight, a pitiable condition, never before seen or heard anywhere; the gift bestowed by the **sons of Bharata who came to rule**; everywhere famine, hunger, disease, armies of the starving—cruelty! Again they are driving the cattle forward. If these cattle are sent to the Legislative Assembly, what will the country become? It will become a cattle shed. It is not “Karunanidhi” who says this. The starving masses crying “Hunger! Hunger!” are asking these questions through the form of Karunanidhi.

It is performing a terrible dance throughout this land.

These “panju!” ministers, who created famine in the country, live in complacent ease without caring about those distressed by famine and without lending an ear even to dreadful reports. Here and there in the country there is a trade in children: parents, driven by hunger and famine, set up “child shops,” name a price, and sell even the sons they themselves bore. ### PDF page 12 — printed page 11

—did those who came to rule ever think, “Why this cruelty? Should this not be stopped? Should we not try to change the conditions that produce it?” They did not think so; not only did they fail to think, did they shed even a tiny drop of tears? No.

These very people, these Congressmen, have now come again asking for votes, carrying the cattle-box.

Should we vote for those of the cattle-box? Is it right to cast our votes for the rulers who poured burning misery into our stomachs, who left all of us tormented by hunger, and raise them once more onto the seat of power? Think about it.

Only if those who care for our welfare, those with the knowledge and ability to meet our basic needs, and those endowed with humanity sit in government and administer the state can people live as people and find a way to live. Is the cattle-box fit for this?

What happens if we vote in the cattle-box?

If we vote in the cattle-box, we shall become people who have set an explosive against ourselves. Yes—we shall become people who have thrown earth into our own stomachs, ruining our own lives.

### PDF page 13 — printed page 12

Even now, the cruelties they commit are unbearable. In Dindigul, people tormented by hunger, without rice and with nothing to eat, wasted away and kept their stomachs going by eating *kattāzhai* as food. From Coimbatore came a cruel report. After eating *kattāzhai* to drive away their hunger and starvation, filling their empty stomachs and surviving for a day or two, six people—six human forms, six sons of Bharat—went to the other world. They died pitiable deaths. Did they do anything about it? No.

In Tirunelveli, gruel troughs have been arranged for those wasting away from hunger. To drink that gruel, to pour the boiling gruel into their cramped, twisting bellies and keep themselves alive to some degree—what a pitiable, deeply pitiable sight! Have you ever thought of the condition of people who walk ten or fifteen miles from all around Tirunelveli, hungry, their legs aching, only to gather there and wait in distress? Did they seek an alternative, a remedy? No!

They did not listen to these things, take notice, correct the situation, offer consolation, or cast even the smallest glance—a compassionate sidelong glance. They have neither the time nor the occasion for any of this! Why?

### PDF page 14 — printed page 13

They have many, many other kinds of work that matter more to them than attending to this. Kamarajar will be somewhere conducting a college closing function. Bhaktavatsalam will be delivering a speech at the opening of a bungalow. Kumarasami Raja will be somewhere delivering a presiding address at a kumbabhishekam ceremony. With such tasks they keep going round and round, always busy. How could they put all that aside and enter into the work of wiping away the people's suffering, of turning back the storm of misery?

It was precisely during the days when people were crying “Hunger, hunger!” that, as I mentioned at the beginning, more than a thousand firings, the confinement of prisoners in jails, and similar acts were taking place.

Prison—prisoners in jail! And among them more than ten thousand political prisoners and preventive detainees. The prison incidents, the degradation taking place inside the prisons, are filled with such enormous horror and cruelty that they set a red fire blazing in our thoughts.

So many prison incidents have occurred. Here is one. In the Travancore region, a man was arrested and confined as a preventive detainee. In prison they rolled him about and threatened him, saying, “Tell the truth—tell the truth; otherwise the kicks will fall!”—

—They tried threatening him and speaking to him. Then they beat him thoroughly. They beat the prisoner beyond the point where he lost awareness of his condition and staggered. They beat him still more, until he curled up, fainted, and fell to the ground.

What anguish must that prisoner's mind have suffered on seeing it. “Alas! Alas, mother!
