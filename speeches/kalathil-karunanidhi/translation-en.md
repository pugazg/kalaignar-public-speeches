Beloved Chairman! Comrades! Elders! Friends of the opposition parties who are standing aside and listening to the speech through the loudspeaker! My greetings to all of you, with thanks.

This meeting, convened here today under the auspices of the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam, has been called in connection with the coming general election: to support Comrade **Albert Jesudhas**, who is contesting the Madras Legislative Assembly from the North Madras Washermenpet constituency after signing an agreement affirming the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam's life-blood question—the separation of Dravida Nadu, the fundamental demand that “Dravida Nadu belongs to the Dravidians”—and thereby receiving the official support and cooperation of our Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam; and, likewise, to support Comrade **James Bond**, who, after signing the same agreement and receiving our support, is contesting for Parliament, the People's House in Delhi, from the constituency covering Washermenpet, Ponneri Taluk, Sriperumbudur, Tiruvallur, Saidapet and other areas—
