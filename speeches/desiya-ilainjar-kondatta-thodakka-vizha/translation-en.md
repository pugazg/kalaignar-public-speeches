## PDF 12 / printed p.11

### National Youth Celebration Inaugural Function

First, I wish to congratulate the Union Ministers S. R. Bommai and Dhanushkodi Adithan, who have arranged this function so splendidly. First, I extend a warm welcome to all of you. My heartfelt greetings. I wish all of you every success in life. I am happy that you have gathered in Tamil Nadu, an ancient and renowned State. Two thousand years ago, this State gave the world the principle of international brotherhood. It was here that the Tamil poet Kaniyan Pungundranar proclaimed to the world:

“Every place is our town; everyone is our kin.”

It was here that the great poet Tiruvalluvar said:

“What country is not his, what town is not his? Why should a man
remain unlearned until death?”

## PDF 13 / printed p.12

Through this couplet he proclaimed to the world that learned people would make every country their own country.

In history and in the epics, heroes are remembered for the achievements they accomplished when they were young.

It was while Pandyan Nedunchezhiyan was young that he fought seven kings at the same time at Talaiyalanganam, defeated them, and earned the name “Pandyan Nedunchezhiyan who won the battle of Talaiyalanganam.”

In the Kurukshetra war, the young Abhimanyu displayed greater valour than Arjuna.

It was while he was Drona's disciple that Arjuna became a renowned warrior.

In the Ramayana, the young Indrajit performed more acts of valour than any other character.

Take, for example, the modern leaders Dr. Ambedkar, Pandit Nehru, Periyar, Anna and Kamaraj. All of them entered public life when they were young.

I would like to recall a story from the Ramayana. Rama, Lakshmana, Bharata and Shatrughna, when they were boys, were using a “cloth ball” and playing with bows and arrows.

## PDF 14 / printed p.13

While they were playing in this way, a cloth ball struck the hunchbacked woman's back. The boys laughed when they saw it. Because of this humiliating incident, the hunchbacked woman began to develop hatred toward Rama.

You all know of the hunchbacked woman's desire for revenge and the events that followed it in that epic. A proverb arose from this incident. It is: “Play turned into trouble.”

But play does not always turn into trouble. It is founded upon health and growth. That is why the poet Bharathi called to children:

“Run and play, child — you
must not sit idle, child.”

Young people from every part of India are here. You have come to reaffirm our commitment to a united India.[^p13-united] You have buried differences of caste, high and low, and language; in you I see a small, beautiful India.

It is most fitting that the National Youth Festival is celebrated on Swami Vivekananda's birthday. He had great faith in the youth in raising India to a higher level.

The body therefore uses the cautious contextual rendering “our commitment to a united India,” while the exact source wording is preserved here; the Tamil archival layer has not been altered.

## PDF 15 / printed p.14

Civilisation is deeply rooted in India. Our motherland taught civilisation and culture to others. In ancient times, seafarers from Tamil Nadu travelled long distances to the East and the West.

Tamil is one of the great languages. It came into being many thousands of years ago. It has served as a foundation for the development of our intellect.

India excels in science and in the arts, in many different fields. We can take pride in our heritage.

“Bharat is an ancient land; you are its children —
never let this thought leave you.”

These are the golden words of Bharathi, the poet and freedom fighter. These words should remind us of our duty. That duty is to bring India back to the state of glory it once enjoyed.

You who are gathered here, and all of us, belong to the period after independence. You are enjoying the fruits of freedom. We obtained freedom through selflessness and sacrifice. Along with safeguarding that freedom, which was won through a hard struggle, we must recreate the glorious condition of the past.

That requires tireless effort. Let us work for it peacefully and courageously. Let us not waste our time and energy on unnecessary matters.

## PDF 16 / printed p.15

Just as birds from different parts of the world gather at Vedanthangal in Tamil Nadu, representatives from all over India have gathered here to fulfil the aims of this festival. Friendship, peace and development are those aims. These principles can be fulfilled only when everyone belonging to humankind is treated equally.

If importance is not given to basic human values, lasting friendship cannot be achieved. A festival of this kind should create a spirit of unity among Indians.

The benefits of development must reach every section of society.

In particular, they must reach the weaker sections. My sincere request is that all of you should help such weaker sections. Energy and effort must be directed into constructive activities. They must be directed into constructive activities in relation to the efforts undertaken by governments to improve the welfare of the poor. This will support the efforts undertaken by governments to improve the welfare of the poor.

Youth possesses tremendous strength and energy. You must engage yourselves in activities that are useful in building the nation. When there is courage joined with firm conviction, the obstacles on the path of progress will disappear like bubbles on water.

## PDF 17 / printed p.16

Temporary attractions must not divert you from your journey toward progress. Temporary gains will not lead to peace and development.

It is my great desire that young people should work with determination toward the ideal of creating India as an energetic and vibrant nation.

Rural youth constitute 78.4 per cent of the total youth population in India. That is why our Tamil Nadu Government has been implementing various schemes for the welfare of rural youth.

During the past two years, more than twenty-five thousand young teachers have been appointed.

Ten thousand young people have been appointed to maintain roads.

Thirteen thousand young people have been appointed as People's Welfare Workers.

Six hundred young doctors have been appointed in government hospitals.

About one thousand young people have been appointed as technical assistants in the Tamil Nadu Electricity Board.

More than ten thousand young men and young women recently joined the Tamil Nadu Police force in our State.

A further ten thousand young men and young women are to be recruited into our police force.

## PDF 18 / printed p.17

A 15 per cent reservation has been provided for rural students in professional courses such as engineering, medicine, dentistry, agriculture, veterinary medicine, law and Siddha medicine.

The freedom fighters have placed the lamp of freedom in the hands of the younger generation. It should not be regarded as a candle. It is a lamp that gives light to crores of people. At this time when the Golden Jubilee of Independence is being celebrated, let us pledge ourselves to accomplish that task.

At this happy moment, I am pleased to inform you that our Government is formulating a “Tamil Nadu Youth Welfare Policy.” I wish everyone gathered here a happy stay here during this festival period.

As today is Vivekananda's birthday, I wish to conclude my speech with his famous words:

“Rise, awake; do not stop
until the goal is reached.”
