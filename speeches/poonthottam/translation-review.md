## Post-freeze re-review (printed p.16 / PDF 17)

Two findings were raised after both layers had been closed. Both fall on the final page, and both were dealt with under the post-freeze rules: documented evidence first, then dependent re-verification.

### Finding A - `மானிடம்` was left untranslated without justification

**Original English:** ``The breeze will glide—the new fragrance will float—`மானிடம்`, the honey-bird, the peacock, the cuckoo, and the *mattuppura* will all create a new artistic feast.``

The accompanying note claimed the word's "intended referent is not certain from the frozen Tamil layer alone."

**Assessment.** The Tamil reading `மானிடம்` is not in dispute and was re-confirmed against the scan during T4. `மானிடம்` is an established Tamil noun meaning *humanity / mankind*. In the sentence it heads the subject list gathered by `எல்லாமே` — `மானிடம், தேன்சிட்டு, மயில், குயில், மாடப்புறா` — all of which "will create a new artistic feast". Reading it as *humanity* is also consistent with the speech as a whole, whose garden is a sustained figure for human society and whose peroration invites `இளைஞர் உலகு`. The earlier note therefore asserted a lexical uncertainty that the source does not support, and leaving Tamil script inside an English sentence implied a corruption that is not present.

**Correction.** `மானிடம்` → **humanity**. The note was removed rather than rewritten, because no source difficulty remains to flag. **The frozen Tamil was not altered.**

### Finding B - `மாட்டுப்புறா` was a transcription defect

The frozen Tamil read `மாட்டுப்புறா`, which T2 Batch 3 had recorded as scan-confirmed, and the English carried the transliteration *mattuppura*.

**Evidence.** See `audit.md` → **T4**. The controlling scan was re-opened with its identity verified (SHA-256 `2a8bf5f6…`, 49,297,657 bytes, 18 pages), PDF 17 was rendered at 400 and 600 dpi, and the word after `குயில்,` shows a single `ட` between `மா` and `ப்`: the print reads **`மாடப்புறா`**. The dictionary expectation did not decide this; the scan did.

**Correction.** Tamil `மாட்டுப்புறா` → **`மாடப்புறா`** (T4, Tamil re-frozen); dependent English *mattuppura* → **dove**.
