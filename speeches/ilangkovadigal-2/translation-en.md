## Ilango Adigal (2)

Speakers here have noted how fitting it is that a statue of Ilango Adigal should stand near the place where Anna rests.

During the World Tamil Conference, under Anna's leadership, statues were erected for Tamil scholars and heroic figures. Even then there was a wish to erect one for Ilango. At later annual Ilango Adigal functions in Chennai, Silambu Chelvar and others pressed for the statue, and I agreed. When they reminded me again this year, I said the statue was ready and would soon be installed.

Today's function is therefore being held with the appropriate leadership, speeches and the greetings of the thousands gathered here.

## PDF 163 / printed p.162

Silambu Chelvar spoke of taking part in occasions that add distinction to Tamil. I am pleased to see people here regardless of party and thank them for that generosity.

Silambu Chelvar's devotion to *Silappathikaram* is well known. Navalar, other Tamil scholars in the DMK, and Anna himself all shared a deep attachment to the epic.

We took *Silappathikaram*, once largely confined to learned circles, into drama and cinema and carried it to the people. At the same time as Ilango's statue is being established here, work is moving rapidly on a *Silappathikaram* art gallery at Kaveripoompattinam.

## PDF 164 / printed p.163

That gallery will tell the epic through sculpture and art—from Kannagi's marriage, through Madurai, to Senguttuvan's northern expedition and the consecration of Kannagi at Vanchi. It is intended to allow even those who have not read the epic to understand its story.

I also report research by Professor Govindarasanar of Karandai Tamil College. Near Surulimalai and the Mangaladevi temple he identified a small two-armed female image and pursued the possibility that it has a connection with the Kannagi tradition.

## PDF 165 / printed p.164

Because that site lies in Kerala, Navalar and I wrote to Kerala Chief Minister Achutha Menon asking that our researchers be allowed to continue the inquiry. He replied that research was already under way and that our researchers could participate.

I hope a firm conclusion will emerge soon.

Silambu Chelvar also spoke about the placing of Ilango's statue near Anna and about ancient Puhar. Critics have even complained that Kamban and Ilango stand near Anna like gatekeepers.

Those who attack us have discovered a new tactic.

## PDF 166 / printed p.165

They now lower us while praising Anna. But that political strategy quickly reveals itself.

Silambu Chelvar regretted that Anna was not alive to see this function. As I see this beautiful gathering, my own heart too dreams that Anna might rise for one moment from the white sands and say: “Brothers, you are doing what I wished.”

Anna sleeps a sleep from which he will not rise, but thousands of Tamil hearts gather in his path and make us feel: “Anna is here.”
