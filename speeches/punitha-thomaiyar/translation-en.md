# Saint Thomas — English translation

Karunanidhi says he attends not only as Chief Minister but also because Little Mount, associated with Thomas, lies in his Saidapet constituency. The gathering itself, he says, answers fears that Christian minority rights might not be protected.

He honours Thomas's mission and condemns responding to ideas with violence rather than ideas. Discussing his own religious position, he says the important question is not whether he accepts God but whether he lives in a way that God would accept him.

The speech then recalls Christian contributions to Tamil through Veeramamunivar, Caldwell and G. U. Pope, and the statues erected for them during the World Tamil Conference period. It closes with Anna's formulation embracing ethical affinities across Christianity, Islam, Hinduism and Buddhism without ritual identification.

## Separate source closing note

Speech delivered at the Saint Thomas festival on 16 April 1972.
