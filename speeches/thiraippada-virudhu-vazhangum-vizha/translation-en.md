## PDF 63 / printed p.62

### Film Awards Presentation Function

Panchu Arunachalam said that whenever an election comes, a film-awards function like this comes just before it. That is not true. The truth is that whenever we arrange an awards function, an election comes along! (loud laughter) That is the truth.

For the purpose of presenting these awards, the films have been viewed over the past five or six months under the chairmanship of Justice Gokul; it has been decided who should receive which prizes; the entire list has been prepared; and just when it was about to be released, the election announcement came. Is this not also an election? Is selecting who should receive the awards not an election too? This election and that election have come together.

This film-awards function is not being held with the election in view. Indeed, precisely so that it should not be used for the election, the M.G.R. Award that was to be presented today has not been presented, in accordance with the Election Commission's wish or order. Because

## PDF 64 / printed p.63

under the Election Commission's rule an award cannot be presented at a government function to a politician who is standing as a candidate in the election, the person who had been announced as the recipient of the M.G.R. Award has not taken part in this function. I am refraining even from saying the name, with political civility, because perhaps even mentioning the name might be contrary to the election rules.

Among the best films, *Indian* has won the first prize. I saw that film. My younger brother Kamal lovingly ordered me to see it. I watched it. I became absorbed in the film—to the point of losing awareness of myself. I wish to say here that there is nothing surprising about that film receiving the first prize.

I did not see *Kadhal Kottai*. I am seventy-four years old; that is why I did not see it. (loud applause) That film has received the second prize. I congratulate it. *Purushan Pondatti* has received the third prize.

*Karuvelam Pookkal* has received a special prize.

For having an outstanding story about women, the film *Gokulathil Seethai* has received another special prize.

There is nothing surprising either in the award for Best Actor going to Kalaignani Kamal.

## PDF 65 / printed p.64

Likewise, there is nothing surprising in the special prize for Best Actor going to my younger brother Vijayakanth.

Among film directors, my younger brother R.K. Selvamani makes and directs revolutionary films with the idealistic resolve to dare anything, not fear censorship, and not mind even if a film is banned because of censorship itself. He has received the Raja Sandow Award, named after the late Raja Sandow, who served as a guide to film directors.

As I mentioned at the outset, Panchu Arunachalam has received the Kannadasan Award.

The Anna Award has gone to my younger brother Liaqat Ali Khan. He has received that award in his capacity as a writer. As I too am a writer, I take pride in his receiving this award.

My younger brother Charlie has received the Kalaivanar Award, named after the late Kalaivanar—the man who stood as a model for what comedy should be and how ideas should be blended into humour, and whose name this auditorium bears.

I join all of you in rejoicing that my younger brother A.R. Rahman, who is sending a flood of music not only across Tamil Nadu and not only across India but through countries around the world, has received the award for Best Music Director.

## PDF 66 / printed p.65

I convey my congratulations to him.

Agathiyan has received the award for Best Director. I heard that his original name is Karunanidhi. (applause) Perhaps he changed his name because he did not want there to be a second Karunanidhi—I do not know! He has received the award for Best Director.

Vikraman has received the award for Best Dialogue Writer. As one who has enjoyed many of his dialogues, I convey my appreciation to him.

Miss Sruthi has been selected as Best Actress. I convey my congratulations to her.

Miss Devayani has received the special prize for Best Actress. I convey my congratulations to her as well.

Kalanjiyam has received the award for Best Story Writer. May my congratulations and appreciation be his.

My younger brother Piraisoodan has been selected for having written good songs for films. My congratulations to him.

Best Villain Actor Prakash Raj—when he received the award here, there was delight, happiness and jubilation throughout this auditorium. I know the reason. Both of us know it. Yes, *Iruvar* know it! (loud applause)

## PDF 67 / printed p.66

Vadivelu has been selected as Best Comedian. Vadivelu need not even act comically. It is enough for him simply to come and stand there—the comedy appears by itself. He is a fine actor capable of expressing humour in every movement. I convey my congratulations to him too.

Success in the film world cannot be obtained merely through one person who participates in it. A director alone, an actor alone, an actress alone, a dialogue writer alone, a music director alone or a playback singer alone—no single individual can achieve a film's success by himself or herself.

For example, a single sculptor is enough to shape a statue. If that sculptor has a chisel and a stone, he can shape a statue. Likewise, for a painter, a little paste, colour and a brush are enough to create a painting. But a film must emerge through the efforts of many people and the labour of many people; it must bring success. In such a great undertaking, it is only when everyone comes together, joins as one and gives their cooperation that unmatched success can be achieved. This is something we have learned through experience.

In that sense, when I see all of you from the film industry in this hall, the happiness I feel,

## PDF 68 / printed p.67

as my younger brother Kamal mentioned—Kamal said this was the house in which I lived; it is the house in which I am living. Please do not, in the meantime, give me retirement from the world of art. (applause) I am a person who writes every day. There is no day on which I do not write. I write precisely to relieve the fatigue that comes upon me. To dispel all the hardships, suffering, weariness and exhaustion that arise for me in politics, the first thing I take up after completing my morning walk is writing. I write every single day. Therefore, do not say “the house in which I lived”; say “the house in which I am living.” Please do not forget that this too is one of my homes.

The problems in this field have also been told to me privately. Because if they were told only privately the public would not know about them, they have gathered all of you together and told me here as well. The people sitting right beside me here could talk among themselves and resolve this film-industry problem. (applause) My younger brother Kalaignani Kamal and our Puratchi Kalaignar, my younger brother Vijayakanth, could, even while I am speaking here now, whisper to each other and find a solution to this problem. (applause) Even so, they have placed it on my head only because they want their elder brother to receive the credit that Karunanidhi resolved it, and for no other reason.

## PDF 69 / printed p.68

Even so, I shall tell them this. Panchu Arunachalam mentioned here that after this election is over I should attend to this matter. Certainly, after the election, toward the end of February, we shall find a final decision—a good decision—for this problem. I express that confidence at this function. As always, this government will continue to work for the world of art and achieve results, because this is a government that functions without treating the world of art as one thing and the government as another. The revenue coming from the world of art is today being used for many schemes carried out by the government. I have not forgotten that crores of rupees come to the government as revenue through the world of art.

Therefore, with that sense of gratitude, as one who belongs there as well as here, I will certainly cooperate with you and find good remedies for your problems. I give that assurance here and take leave of you with the confidence that you will consider this assurance better than the awards and prizes presented here.
